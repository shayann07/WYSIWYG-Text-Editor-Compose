# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different languages,
backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Discuss code and evidence
without harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or disclosure of private
information. Give constructive feedback and respect other people's time.

The maintainer may edit/remove inappropriate content, limit participation or
ban repeated or severe violations. Report concerns privately to @shayann07
through an available GitHub private channel. Do not publish identifying
details of affected people. Enforcement decisions should protect participants
and be proportionate to the conduct and context.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions
and other project spaces. It does not replace the contributor accountability
and human-authorship requirements in CONTRIBUTING.md.
